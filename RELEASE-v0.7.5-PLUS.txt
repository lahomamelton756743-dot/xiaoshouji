Little Phone v0.7.5 Plus

Front-end rework based on the v0.7.5 hotfix / v0.7.4 visual base:
- Home header compressed; Together uses a raster calligraphy asset so Android WebView no longer falls back to a plain italic font.
- Decorative flowers stay behind the relationship day count instead of covering it.
- GPT pendant is integrated into the listening card near the GPT avatar, not pasted onto the outside edge.
- Diary has no separate cover. The entire Diary page is the diary paper; opening Diary lands on the newest entry.
- Diary left/right page-turn animations use opposite physical directions correctly.
- GPT pen/bookmark is used as a bottom-right page-edge decoration.
- Xiaomi Health dashboard is larger and shows available sleep duration, score, steps, heart rate, distance, calories, sleep time, wake time, progress bars, and a light data-scale visualization.
- GPT night window has its own footer decoration space on Status so it cannot be hidden behind the phone-status card.
- The removed bottom pair-status cards stay removed.
- GPT memory/profile MCP work from v0.7.5 is retained.
- Custom backgrounds remain phone-local only.
