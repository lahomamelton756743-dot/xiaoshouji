Little Phone v0.8.1 preview6

Changes:
- retuned diary notebook frame: taller, visually centered, same frame for cover and inner pages
- status bar flushes to the safe-area top more tightly
- added desktop edit mode: long press on desktop to enter editing
- apps can be repositioned by selecting them and tapping a new empty cell or a Dock slot
- added widget picker and system widget picker
- brought legacy glass widgets into the new desktop system: clock/weather, together days, music, health, calendar, todo, phone status, memory, paper, mail
- widgets can be added, moved, and removed locally
- added desktop layout persistence and reset-to-default action
